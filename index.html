What these agencies do

They're private businesses connected directly to the MVA system. They handle new registrations, renewals, title transfers, duplicate titles, temporary tags and plate returns. You pay the same state fees as at the MVA plus the agency's service fee.

Tag & Title Finder is an independent directory. We are not the Maryland MVA and are not affiliated with any agency listed. Agencies set their own service fees; always confirm hours and prices by phone.
