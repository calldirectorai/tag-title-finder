What these agencies do

They're private businesses authorized to process MVA transactions. Depending on the agency, that can include new registrations, renewals, title transfers, duplicate titles, temporary tags and plate returns. You pay the state's charges plus the agency's own service fee.

Tag & Title Finder is an independent directory. We are not the Maryland MVA and are not a government website. Any listed agency that is a client of CallDirector AI, which runs this site, is labeled as one. Agencies set their own service fees; always confirm hours and prices by phone.
