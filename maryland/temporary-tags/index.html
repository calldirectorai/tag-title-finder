Temporary tags (30-day registration)

Need to drive a car to its safety inspection before it has permanent plates? Maryland offers a one-time 30-day temporary registration.

The MVA offers a 30-day temporary registration when you need plates to get the vehicle safety inspected. Private tag and title agencies can also issue them.

You only get one. The MVA says only one 30-day temporary registration may be issued, so apply when the car is actually ready for inspection. If it needs repairs first, the MVA says you can title the vehicle now and apply for the temporary registration later.

What the MVA asks for

  • A completed Temporary Inspection Waiver (form VR-129)
  • Maryland insurance on the vehicle
  • Your title paperwork, if you are titling the car at the same time
  • Payment for the temporary registration fee and any title fees and taxes due

Once the car passes inspection, you apply for regular one- or two-year plates.

Summarized from the Maryland MVA, checked September 2026: Register a Vehicle in Maryland. Requirements change and depend on your situation; the MVA's pages are the final word.

Before you call an agency, ask

  • Do you issue 30-day temporary registrations today?
  • What is your service fee, and what is the total with state charges?
  • What should I bring for my situation (private sale, dealer, out of state)?
  • Can I come back to you for permanent plates after inspection?
